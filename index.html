Sahaayak (सहायक) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Sahaayak ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Nepal government document helper app developed by Team Sahaayak. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Sahaayak is designed to work completely offline and collects minimal data. We collect and store the following information only on your device:

  • Your language preference (English or Nepali)
  • Your document checklists (which documents you have ticked)
  • Your saved/bookmarked services
  • App theme preferences

All of this data is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to our servers or any third party.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device's contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Any personal identification information
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Any data that can identify you personally

3. Firebase (Remote Data)

Our app may connect to Google Firebase Firestore to fetch updated service information (such as new government services, updated fees, or document requirements). This connection only downloads public data to your device. We do not upload any personal information to Firebase.

Firebase's privacy policy can be found at: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Internet Permission

The app requests internet permission only to fetch updated government service data from our Firebase database. The app works fully offline if no internet connection is available — all core features use locally stored data.

5. Phone Call Permission

The app requests phone call permission only to allow you to directly call government offices by tapping the "Call Office" button. We do not initiate calls automatically or record any call information.

6. Third-Party Services

When you use the "Share via WhatsApp" feature, your data is shared directly through WhatsApp's own service. We do not store or process the content of your WhatsApp messages. WhatsApp's privacy policy applies to that interaction.

When you tap "Find government office near me", you are redirected to Google Maps. Google's privacy policy applies to that interaction.

7. Data Storage and Security

All data saved by Sahaayak (checklists, preferences, bookmarks) is stored using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage). This data is private to your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We have no access to this data.

8. Children's Privacy

Sahaayak is a general public service app suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from any users, children's data is equally protected.

9. Data Source Disclaimer

All government service information in Sahaayak has been sourced from Kathmandu Metropolitan City records. We apologize for any errors or changes in data. For the most accurate and up-to-date information, please contact your nearest government office. Sahaayak is not an official government application.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our end. You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by uninstalling Sahaayak from your device, or by going to Settings → Clear All Checklists within the app.
